|---|---:|---:|---|

**The finding that recurs:** models agree with **each other** (κ ≈ 0.5–0.6) far more than with the **human**
(κ ≤ 0.30 at Stage 3). Agreement between models is therefore no proxy for validity. That is the basis for human
confirmation of every Core (Sets G and H: 428 human confirmation decisions).
